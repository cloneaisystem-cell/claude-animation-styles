# /flight-path — the globe spins, the flight draws itself

Animate a flight from Bucharest to Tokyo on a 3D globe. Use a clay-white globe with real coastlines and soft light. The globe spins in and settles on Bucharest. A blue great-circle arc draws itself, and a small jet rides its tip. A big distance counter at the top runs from 0 to 8,877 km in step with the jet. Then cut to a chase camera behind the jet. At the end, crane up over Japan as a red pin drops onto Tokyo with a squash and bounce, and the counter turns blue.
