# /origami — one sheet becomes a crane

Animate one square sheet of red paper folding itself into a crane, in 3D. Use a warm off-white paper backdrop, soft light from the top left and a soft shadow under the paper. The sheet has a red front, a cream back and fine paper fibres. It folds in crisp, snappy steps along visible creases: diagonal, triangle, square base, bird base. Then cut to a low three-quarter angle. The neck and tail rise, the head bends down and the wings open. The crane flaps twice and lifts off out of frame, leaving its shadow behind.
