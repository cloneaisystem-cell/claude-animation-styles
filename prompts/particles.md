# /particles — 10,000 dots find a face

Animate my profile picture out of 10,000 dots. Start with a cream dot matrix where each dot's size follows the photo's brightness, so the face is already readable. Then the dots peel off, swirl and fly back. On the beat, they snap into the full-colour portrait with a flash. Then push in slowly: the portrait breathes, a rim of light sweeps from the crown to the chin, and the dots stream out to the right.
