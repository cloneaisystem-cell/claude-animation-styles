# /front-page — the paper spins in

Animate an old movie newspaper spin. A broadsheet spins in from the dark, fast, and slams flat to camera with a little bounce. The masthead reads "THE DAILY PROMPT". The headline reads "NINE STYLES. NINE PROMPTS." over a halftone photo, with columns of small serif text. After the slam, the camera drifts in, the paper settles with a slight curl, and dust motes float in a light beam.
