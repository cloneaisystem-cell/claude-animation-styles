# /watercolor — pigment blooms on paper

Animate a watercolour painting appearing on rough cold-press paper. Wet pigment blooms outward in soft, uneven edges. First comes a warm sunset wash in red and ochre, then a darker ridge of hills, then a small red sun. The edges darken as the pigment dries, and a few granulation specks settle in the paper's texture. Finish with a fine hand-lettered title: "slow mornings".
