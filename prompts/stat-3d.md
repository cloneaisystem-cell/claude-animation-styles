# /stat-3d — the number that climbs

Animate a 3D growth stat for a made-up bakery called "Orbit Bakery". A big extruded number counts up from 0 to +248% with a slight bounce, standing on a glossy floor. Behind it, 3D bars rise one after another, the last one tallest and red. Add a small label: "weekend orders, Jan → Jun". Show soft studio light and reflections on the floor, and orbit the camera slowly.
